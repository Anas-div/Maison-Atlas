About Maison Atlas

Our story

Home/ About

Since 2012

Rooted in craft, designed for today

Maison Atlas began as a two-person workshop in the Habous quarter. Today we are a team of designers, project managers and 28 partner artisans, creating homes and commercial spaces across Casablanca.

We believe good design is warm, honest and made to last — zellige, cedar and brass paired with clean modern lines.

— Nadia El Fassi, Founder
